About Sterns Limited

Your trusted partner in professional garment care

Who We Are

Sterns Limited has been proudly serving the Halifax community with professional dry cleaning, laundry, and garment care services. We understand that your clothing is an investment, and we treat every garment with the care and attention it deserves.

Our Commitment to Quality

We are committed to providing exceptional service quality and customer satisfaction. Our team uses industry-leading techniques and the finest cleaning methods to ensure your garments are returned in pristine condition. Whether it's a delicate wedding gown, professional business attire, or everyday clothing, we have the expertise to care for all fabric types.

Services We Offer

  • Dry Cleaning - Professional care for suits, dresses, outerwear, and formal wear
  • Laundry Services - Expert laundering with careful pressing and finishing
  • Alterations & Tailoring - Expert seamstresses for custom fits and repairs
  • Wedding Gown Care - Specialized preservation and cleaning for special occasion wear
  • Household Items - Professional cleaning for comforters, curtains, blankets, and more
  • Specialty Cleaning - Custom care for delicate and hard-to-clean fabrics

Why Choose Sterns Limited?

  • Experienced team dedicated to quality and customer service

  • Fair pricing and reliable turnaround times
  • State-of-the-art cleaning facilities and methods
  • Personalized care for all garment types
