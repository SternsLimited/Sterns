About Sterns Limited

Welcome to Sterns Limited, your trusted partner for professional dry cleaning and laundry services in Charlottetown, PEI. We have decades of experience and a commitment to having good vibes only.

  • Dry Cleaning - Professional care for suits, dresses, outerwear, and formal wear
  • Laundry Services - Expert laundering with careful pressing and finishing
  • Alterations & Tailoring - Expert seamstresses for custom fits and repairs
  • Wedding Gown Care - Specialized preservation and cleaning for special occasion wear

  • Specialty Cleaning - Custom care for delicate and hard-to-clean fabrics
