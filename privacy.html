Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us

Effective Date: January 2025

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Sterns Limited, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information from you:

  • Name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Billing information (if applicable)
  • Service and order history
  • Communications with our business

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and manage your dry cleaning orders
  • Notify you about order status and pickup availability
  • Respond to customer service requests
  • Send appointment reminders and service updates
  • Send promotional messages only if you have opted in
  • Improve our services

SMS Communications

If you opt in to receive SMS messages, we may send order notifications, reminders, customer service updates, and promotional messages (where applicable).

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe.
  • Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Your consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing our services.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Information Sharing

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business (such as payment processors or SMS service providers) solely for the purpose of providing our services. These providers are required to protect your information and may not use it for their own marketing.

We may also disclose information when required by law.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Update your contact information
  • Access the personal information we maintain about you, where applicable by law
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal or operational recordkeeping requirements
  • Withdraw your SMS consent at any time by replying STOP

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on our website with a revised effective date.
